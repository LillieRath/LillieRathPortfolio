    After completing this assignment, I learned how to properly set up simple lines and proper breaks or spaces between each paragraph. Before starting this assignment, I had know quite a bit about hierarchy from previous classes that focused on textual hierarchies and disruption of text to add emphasis to specific words or phrases. The way I used hierarchy in my webpage was by adding headers to the more important text such as titles, chapters, and text that I felt held a lot of impact. I then used italics, bold, strong, and emphasis tags to add emphasis on words that described a type of feeling or words I wanted to add a feeling to, to give the phrases a more poetic read rather than a book type of read. I applied what I learned from the lesson by really trying to utilize the tags properly and using them more than once. I think overall, I really like how the entire webpage looks but I think the second phrase could use some work, but due to how short the phrase is, I was having a harder time figuring how to emphasize parts of it to make it work and fit well with what is being said. I don't think, though, that I would change anything about the webpage other than adding some sort of decor and maybe some color to give the texts more feeling. After completing this assignment, I feel that I can confidently say that I can understand the role designers have in delivering information, as it is something that designers have to be able complete proficiently to convey the right meaning that a customer is wanting to have conveyed.
